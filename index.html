Edge sign language recognition: measuring the whole pipeline

M.S. thesis, NTUST, 2026

Papers on efficient continuous sign language recognition report the cost of the recognition model alone. I deployed every stage on edge hardware to find out where the time and energy actually go, then cut it.

  • Converted every neural stage to TensorRT and profiled latency, memory and per-stage energy on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.
  • Showed a released recognition model breaks on a different pose estimator (unnormalized keypoint confidence), and fixed it with fine-tuning.
  • Tested a distilled student with an RGB-aware teacher against a retrained control, with 95% paired bootstrap intervals.

Self-supervised ViTs for chest X-ray diagnosis with few labels

Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine (Springer Nature), 2026. Second author of four.

Detecting stroke and heart failure from chest X-rays when only a small labeled set exists, by adapting a general DINOv2 Vision Transformer to X-ray images before training the classifier.

  • Designed the domain-adaptation stage: froze the bottom 8 of 12 ViT blocks to keep general visual features while the top blocks learn X-ray structure.
  • Ran the ablations that show each pretraining stage's contribution on both tasks.

AI surrogates for semiconductor chamber profiles

NTUST × TSMC research collaboration, 2026–present

Running a chamber simulator for every candidate recipe is slow. A trained surrogate predicts a wafer's 100-point thickness profile in milliseconds, so engineers can search recipes interactively.

  • Generated training data from a rotation-aware physics model and compared four ways of compressing the profile target.
  • Benchmarked six surrogate models, including MLP, 1D-CNN, XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost.
  • Built a 3D digital twin of the chamber that shows predictions in real time.

Boiler furnace pressure control

PLN Indonesia Power, PLTU Jeranjang (3×25 MW coal-fired plant), 2023–2024

Furnace pressure was held by operators adjusting the induced draft fans by hand from the control room. I led a two-person instrumentation team to replace that with a closed loop.

Safe window
−200 Pa−140−400
Too low: implosion riskToo high: hot gas escapes
  • Designed PID control on a HollySys HOLLiAS MACS DCS, averaging two pressure transmitters.
  • Added a fallback: if readings fluctuate or pass a limit, the loop hands control back to operators.
  • Commissioned on the plant; pressure stayed within −140 to −40 Pa.

Automated NPK fertilization system

Undergraduate thesis, ITS, 2021–2022. Published in Jurnal Teknik ITS.

A closed-loop system that reads soil nutrient levels and doses nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium solutions to hold them at a setpoint, with remote monitoring from a phone.

  • Wrote ESP32 firmware (ESP-IDF) that reads an NPK sensor over RS-485 and drives three peristaltic pumps with PID control.
  • Designed the main controller and pump driver PCBs in EAGLE, then assembled and tested them.
  • Added remote monitoring and manual override through Blynk.
